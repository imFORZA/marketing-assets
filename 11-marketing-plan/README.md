# Marketing Plan: One-Page Plan, Worksheet, and Monthly Review

A companion template to our guide [How to Build a Small Business Marketing Plan From the Customers You Actually Need](https://www.imforza.com/blog/small-business-marketing-plan/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=small-business-marketing-plan). It sits next to the 10 M10 folders as a working tool. It isn't an 11th asset.

## What this folder does

This folder turns one number, the revenue you need from new customers in the next 12 months, into a plan that fits on one page. You work backwards from that number:

1. Revenue goal ÷ what one new customer is worth in year one = customers you need.
2. Customers ÷ your close rate = leads you need.
3. Leads ÷ your website conversion rate = visits you need.
4. Visits split across a few channels, each with a monthly target and a person responsible.
5. A budget ceiling set from what one customer is worth, not from a percentage of revenue.
6. Four numbers you check once a month: visits, leads, customers, and cost per customer.

Every line on the finished page traces back to the revenue number. When a channel misses, you know how many customers that miss costs you.

- [`ONE-PAGE-MARKETING-PLAN.md`](ONE-PAGE-MARKETING-PLAN.md): the blank one-page plan to copy, plus the "current as of" box and changelog that keep it honest.

## Three ways to use it

**1. By hand with these files.** Open the worksheet, run Steps 1 to 6 with a calculator, and copy each answer into the one-page plan. Look at the completed example whenever a blank isn't obvious. We'd encourage you to do it by hand at least once so the math is yours.

**2. In the Google Sheet.** [Make your own copy of the FREE One-Page Marketing Plan & Scoreboard Sheet](https://imforza.link/marketing-plan-sheet). Fill in the yellow cells on the Inputs tab and the Plan tab does the math. The Monthly Scoreboard tab has a row for each month, finds the first number that missed, and keeps a year-to-date total.

**3. With the Builder.** [Build your plan with the FREE Marketing Plan Builder](https://www.imforza.com/resources/marketing-plan-builder/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=small-business-marketing-plan). It runs the same math, checks your budget ceiling against benchmark costs for your industry, labels any placeholder inputs, and hands back the plan as a PDF, as Markdown, and as a Google Sheet with the scoreboard built in.

All three use the same formulas and the same rounding rule, so a plan built one way matches a plan built another way.

## Build order inside this folder

1. Work through `CUSTOMER-MATH-WORKSHEET.md` first. If you don't know your close rate or conversion rate yet, use a placeholder and mark it.
2. Copy the answers into `ONE-PAGE-MARKETING-PLAN.md` and put a name next to every channel.
3. Put the monthly review on your calendar and start the log in `MONTHLY-REVIEW.md` the following month.

## Keeping the plan current

The method doesn't change. The numbers you plug into it do.

- **Monthly:** fill in the four numbers, find the first miss, and change one thing.
- **Quarterly:** re-run the full math in the worksheet with your measured close and conversion rates replacing any placeholders, then rebalance the channel table.
- **Every re-run:** check the "current as of" box at the bottom of your plan before you check the math. Replace any benchmark, tool, or channel that changed and update the date.
- **Log it:** add a line to the changelog under your plan (date, what changed, why). If you keep your plan in a fork of this repo, log the change in this folder's `CHANGELOG.md` too.
