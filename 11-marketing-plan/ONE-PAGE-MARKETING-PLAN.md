# One-Page Marketing Plan

Copy the block below into a document, a notes app, or your own fork of this repo. It's the whole plan: target, math, channels, budget, scoreboard, and review.

## How to fill it

- **Every blank comes from the step with the same number.** The TARGET lines come from Steps 1 and 2, MATH from Steps 3 and 4, and so on. [`CUSTOMER-MATH-WORKSHEET.md`](CUSTOMER-MATH-WORKSHEET.md) walks through each step with the formula.
- **Fill it top to bottom.** Each section uses the answers above it.
- **Mark your placeholders.** If your close rate or conversion rate is a guess, tick the placeholder box and write the date you'll replace it with a measured number. A labeled guess is fine. An unlabeled one quietly becomes a fact.
- **Put a name next to every channel.** A person, not a department. Unowned channels don't get worked.
- **The channel TOTAL must equal your visits needed per month.**
- **Stuck on a blank?** Compare it with the completed plan in [`EXAMPLE-marlowe-voss-family-law.md`](EXAMPLE-marlowe-voss-family-law.md).

## Current as of

Keep this box at the bottom of your plan. At each quarterly re-run, check the box before you check the math, replace anything that changed, and update the date.
