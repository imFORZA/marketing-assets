## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-29

### Added

- `README.md`: what the folder does, the file list, three ways to use it (by hand, the Google Sheet, the Marketing Plan Builder), and how to keep the plan current.
- `ONE-PAGE-MARKETING-PLAN.md`: the blank one-page plan from the companion guide, with fill-in notes, a "current as of" box, and a three-line changelog.
- `EXAMPLE-marlowe-voss-family-law.md`: a completed plan for a fictional two-attorney family law firm, with notes on where a real firm pulls each number from.
- `CUSTOMER-MATH-WORKSHEET.md`: Steps 1 to 6 as fill-in lines with formulas, the rounding rule shared with the Builder and the Sheet, placeholder guidance, benchmark tables, and versions for a boutique inn, a real estate team, a restaurant, and an online store.
- `MONTHLY-REVIEW.md`: the 30-minute monthly review, the quarterly re-run, and a 12-month log that mirrors the Sheet's Monthly Scoreboard tab.
- `PROMPTS.md`: three single-line prompts for any AI assistant (check my math, channel task list, run my monthly review).
