# Monthly Review

A plan you don't check is a wish. This review keeps yours alive in 30 minutes a month: same day every month, same four numbers.

## The four numbers

| Number | Where it comes from | Monthly target |
|---|---|---|
| Visits to money pages | GA4 landing page report, filtered to service, contact, and booking pages | from Step 4 |
| Leads | Form submissions + tracked calls + bookings (GA4 key events or your CRM) | from Step 3 |
| Customers | Closed-won in your CRM, or your invoicing system | from Step 2 |
| Cost per customer | Total marketing spend (dollars + hours at a rate) ÷ new customers | from Step 6 |

Four numbers, not a dashboard with forty. If you want a fifth, make it conversion rate (leads ÷ visits), because it's the earliest warning you'll get that something on your site broke.

## The 30-minute review

**1. Fill in the four numbers for last month next to their targets.** Give it 10 minutes. Here's [the fastest way to pull them](https://www.imforza.com/blog/how-to-measure-marketing-roi/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=small-business-marketing-plan).

**2. Find the first number that missed, reading top to bottom.** The first miss tells you where to look:

| First miss | What it usually means | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| Visits | A channel problem | The channel table tells you which owner to talk to |
| Leads (visits hit) | A website problem | The pages people land on: the next step, the form, the phone number, the proof |
| Customers (leads hit) | A sales or response-speed problem | How fast and how well each lead gets a reply |
| Cost per customer (customers hit) | A channel mix problem | Which channels cost the most per customer they produce |
| Nothing missed | The plan is working | Keep going, and resist the urge to change things |

**3. Change one thing.** Not five. One channel's tactic, one page, or one response process. Write down what you changed and why in the log below.

**4. Re-run a step if a number has been wrong for three months running.** Three months is the point where a miss stops being noise. Go back to the step in [`CUSTOMER-MATH-WORKSHEET.md`](CUSTOMER-MATH-WORKSHEET.md) that produced the number and run it again with what you've measured.

## Quarterly re-run

Once a quarter, add 30 more minutes:

1. Check the "current as of" box on your plan and replace any benchmark, tool, or channel that changed.
2. Re-run the whole customer math with your measured close and conversion rates replacing any placeholders.
3. Rebalance the channel table so it still adds up to your monthly visit target.
4. Add a line to your plan's changelog: date, what changed, why.

Your first-quarter numbers will be wrong. That's fine. The plan is a forecast that gets better every quarter, not a prophecy.
