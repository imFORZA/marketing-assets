# 06, SEO and Keyword Strategy

The Market Layer closes here. You know what you sell. Now you write down the searches you want to win.

## What this layer does

This folder is the single source of truth for how you are found. Keywords, topic clusters, content gaps, and the rules you follow to show up in Google, Bing, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Copilot. Every piece of content your team or your agents produce is briefed from this folder.

## Files in this folder

- `KEYWORDS.md`, Primary, secondary, and long-tail keyword lists. Search intent tags. SERP-feature targets. A research log.
- `TOPIC-CLUSTERS.md`, The hub-and-spoke content plan. Pillar pages and supporting posts. Internal-link architecture.
- `GEO-AEO.md`, Generative Engine Optimization and Answer Engine Optimization. How to structure content for AI citations across ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Bing Copilot.

## Build order inside this layer

1. Start with `KEYWORDS.md`. Ground every term in a real buyer question.
2. Build `TOPIC-CLUSTERS.md`. Wire one pillar page to eight to twelve supporting posts.
3. Finish with `GEO-AEO.md`. Apply the entity clarity and schema rules on the way out.

## What this folder feeds into

- `07-website` uses the cluster map to shape site architecture and internal links.
- `08-content` briefs every article, video, and podcast from this folder.
- `10-brand-brain` loads the keyword and topic map into any content-writing agent.

After this folder is filled in, move to `07-website` to define the pages that carry the work.
