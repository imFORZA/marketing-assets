# LinkedIn Playbook

Part of M10: The Marketing Foundation 10 by imFORZA.

## Purpose

LinkedIn is where B2B buyers go to watch peers and vendors in semi-public. Our goal is to be worth watching. We post useful, specific, practitioner content that moves the brand forward even when nobody clicks a link.

## Format rules

- **Post length:** 120 to 180 words for a single post. 600 to 1,200 words for an article.
- **Line length:** 1 to 3 sentences per paragraph. Use single line breaks for rhythm.
- **First line:** Pull the reader in before "See more." No "Excited to share." No "Humbled to announce."
- **Emojis:** None.
- **Hashtags:** 0 to 3 at the bottom. Never mid-sentence.
- **Links:** External links go in the first comment, not in the post body.
- **Tagging:** Only tag people or companies when the post is materially about them and they will want to be tagged.

## Cadence

- **Company page:** 3 posts per week.
- **Founder voice:** 3 posts per week.
- **Alternate days** between company and founder so the feed does not collide.
- **Articles:** 1 per month, tied to a pillar post on the site.

## Voice modulation

Inherits from `/02-voice/VOICE.md`. Apply these LinkedIn-specific dials.

- **Founder voice:** 70 percent field notes (what I saw today, what a customer told me, what I got wrong), 20 percent opinion, 10 percent proof.
- **Company voice:** 60 percent educational, 30 percent culture and hiring, 10 percent proof.
- **Formality:** Low-medium.
- **Energy:** Medium. Never hyped.

## Post structures that work

### Field note

```
Last [day] I was on a call with [real role].

They said: "[quote]."

Here is what I am taking away.

[Insight 1]
[Insight 2]
[Insight 3]

[Punchy close.]
```

### Contrarian claim

```
Most [role]s think [common belief].

I think that is backwards.

Here is why.

[Reason 1]
[Reason 2]
[Reason 3]

[Named example with specific number.]

[Single-sentence reframe.]
```

### Teaching

```
Three things I have learned about [topic] after [number] engagements.

One. [Lesson.]
Two. [Lesson.]
Three. [Lesson.]

[Named example that proves the lesson.]

[CTA in the first comment.]
```

### Honest lesson

```
I made a [mistake] last [period]. Here is what happened.

[Setup.]
[The error.]
[The cost.]

Here is what I would do differently.

[Fix 1]
[Fix 2]

[Invitation to comment with the reader's version.]
```

## Comment strategy

- **On your own posts:** Respond to every comment in the first 24 hours. Ask a follow-up question when possible.
- **On peer posts:** Comment where your voice adds something. Never "great post," never dropped links.
- **In DMs:** Only when someone has opted into a conversation. No cold DMs with a pitch.

## Company page vs. personal profile

- Personal profiles see 5 to 10 times the reach of company pages on LinkedIn.
- Use the company page for culture, hiring, proof posts, and anchored repurposed content.
- Use founder profiles for opinion, field notes, and first-person teaching.
- Share company-page posts from the founder profile within the first 2 hours when it matters.

## Image and video

- Square 1:1 or portrait 4:5 images perform best.
- Native video out-performs external links.
- Captions burned in. 60 to 90 seconds for talking-head.
- Style pulls from `/03-visual/IMAGERY.md`.

## Tracking

- Every post that points to the site uses a UTM set per `/09-data/TRACKING-PLAN.md`.
- Log top-performers in `/09-data/TOP-PERFORMERS.md` monthly.

## Banned patterns

- "Thrilled to announce"
- "Humbled to share"
- "Here are 10 tips for..."
- Multi-line motivational quotes with a sunset photo
- Repost-only weeks
- "Hot take" posts that are not actually contrarian
- "Agree?" as the closing line

## Example posts

### Field note

```
Yesterday a home-services owner told me something that stuck.

He said, "We have been burned by three agencies in four years. If the fourth sends me another report full of impressions, I am going in-house."

Here is what I am taking away.

Small business owners do not want impressions. They want booked jobs.

If the agency cannot show which blog post drove which job, the agency is selling activity.

If you work in SMB marketing and your dashboard does not start with booked revenue, you are one slow quarter away from getting fired.
```

### Teaching

```
Three things I have learned about AI content after 60 plus small-business engagements.

One. AI tools do not produce on-brand content. They produce on-average content. The gap is your brand foundation.

Two. If your ICP, voice, offer, and proof are not written down, every AI tool will guess. Badly.

Three. The small business winners in the AI era are the ones who wrote their foundation down first. The tools do not matter if the inputs are empty.

We wrapped a 60-day foundation audit for Northstar Home Services in March. First month live, 47 organic leads. Not 4.7. 47.

If you want the 10-asset starter we used to build theirs, comment "pack" and I will DM it.
```
