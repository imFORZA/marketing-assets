# X / Twitter Playbook

Part of M10: The Marketing Foundation 10 by imFORZA.

## Purpose

X is the fastest surface to test a hook and compound a thought. We post on X to sharpen ideas in public and to build a small, real audience of practitioners who care about the craft.

## Format rules

- **Short post:** 200 to 270 characters.
- **Thread:** 8 to 12 tweets. First tweet is the hook. Last tweet offers the reader something to do.
- **No emojis.**
- **No hashtags.** X rarely rewards them. Use them only when piggybacking on a live event.
- **Links:** Links go in a reply, not in the first tweet of a thread.
- **Mentions:** Mention only when tagging helps the person and the context.

## Cadence

- Daily short posts. 2 to 4 per day.
- 1 thread per week, 8 to 12 tweets.
- 1 engagement sprint per week where the account replies to 10 to 20 peer posts.
- No automated posting of the same thread to multiple platforms.

## Hook patterns

- Single stat + one-line explanation.
- Contrarian claim + first sentence of the defense.
- Observation + promise to explain in the thread.
- Quoted customer line + context.

## Thread structure

1. **Hook:** 200 to 270 characters. Promise what the thread will deliver.
2. **Why it matters:** One tweet explaining stakes.
3. **Insight tweets:** 5 to 8 tweets, one idea per tweet, numbered.
4. **Proof:** One tweet with a named example.
5. **Recap:** One tweet summarizing the spine.
6. **CTA:** Invite the reader to reply, follow, or grab a linked asset from a reply.

## Reply strategy

- Reply where you add value. Never "great post."
- Save long takes for a quote tweet or a follow-up thread.
- Never drop a link in a reply on someone else's post.

## Brand vs. founder

- Brand account: curated, educational, slower cadence.
- Founder account: field notes, opinion, daily cadence.
- Prioritize founder account for growth. Brand account for repurposing and archiving.

## Voice modulation

- Dry, direct, specific.
- Shorter sentences than LinkedIn.
- Humor is okay if it does not require a joke format (no setups with punchlines).
- Avoid "hot take." Be wrong in a useful direction if you must be wrong.

## Banned patterns

- Long threads that say nothing until tweet 7.
- "You should be..." imperatives without evidence.
- Quote-tweeting to dunk.
- Posting the same thread every three months with a different hook.
- Screenshots of GPT output passed off as insight.

## Tracking

- Log thread URL and impressions in `/09-data/TOP-PERFORMERS.md` weekly.
- UTM any link in a reply per `/09-data/TRACKING-PLAN.md`.
