# Quora Playbook

Part of M10: The Marketing Foundation 10 by imFORZA.

## Purpose

Quora remains an underrated surface for small and growing businesses. Answer engines cite it. Buyers search it. A well-written answer ranks on Google for years.

## Format rules

- **Length:** 400 to 700 words.
- **Structure:** Question, direct answer, supporting points, named example, link back.
- **Linking:** One link to the site, used sparingly and only when the linked page actually answers the reader's next question.
- **Images:** One optional image or embed. Never required.
- **Voice:** Teacher mode. Specific. Never self-promotional before the value lands.

## Cadence

- 2 answers per week.
- Each answer maps to a primary or secondary keyword from `/06-seo/KEYWORDS.md`.
- Each answer is a unique version of an existing blog post, not a copy-paste.

## Answer shape

1. **Direct answer:** 1 to 3 sentences. Give the answer first. Buyers reward clarity.
2. **Why:** 1 to 2 short paragraphs. Explain the reasoning.
3. **How:** A numbered list of 3 to 6 steps or a checklist.
4. **Example:** A named example with a number.
5. **Close:** One sentence that invites the reader to the site if they want to go deeper.

## Question selection

Pick questions that match your target keywords. Prioritize questions that already have views but no strong answer.

- Questions with 10 to 1,000 daily views are ideal.
- Questions older than 6 months with active traffic are better than brand-new questions.
- Questions with a single weak top answer are the highest leverage.

## Do and do not

**Do**

- Answer the question asked. Not an adjacent question.
- Cite sources when you use a number.
- Share a contrarian view if you have one that you can defend.
- Re-answer every 12 months if the answer is out of date.

**Do not**

- Paste a blog post verbatim.
- Start with "I am the founder of..."
- Promise a free download in the first paragraph.
- Argue with other answerers publicly.

## Example answer

**Question:** "How do small HVAC companies in Austin compete with Yelp ads?"

**Answer:**

You compete by owning the organic search and AI-overview real estate that your buyers actually use.

Yelp ads are paid. They end the day you stop paying. Organic positions in Google, Google's AI Overview, ChatGPT search, and Perplexity compound. Three months of work on the right content produces years of compounding reach.

Here is how small HVAC companies in Austin can do it without a big agency budget.

1. Write one pillar page on "residential HVAC in Austin" that is 2,500 plus words, answers the five questions buyers actually type, and links to 6 to 8 supporting spoke posts.
2. Add FAQ blocks with visible answers, 40 to 80 words each, and wire `FAQPage` schema.
3. Add a named author bio page with Person schema. Answer engines cite real people, not "the team."
4. Claim the Google Business Profile, attach 10 plus photos, and collect 20 plus first-party reviews that mention service types and neighborhoods.
5. Track every inbound lead to a UTM-tagged URL so you can see which pages produce booked jobs.

A real example. Northstar Home Services is a 45-person HVAC shop in Austin. They shipped the foundation above in 60 days. First month after launch they booked 47 organic leads. The month before launch they booked 12. Yelp ads in that market had been producing 6 to 8 leads per month at a declining conversion rate.
