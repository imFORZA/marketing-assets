# Taste

Part of M10: The Marketing Foundation 10 by imFORZA. One of the three [creative context](README.md) files.

## How to use this file

- **What goes in:** Your quality bar, written as rules with reasons. Built from work you admire and from the drafts you rejected.
- **How agents read it:** Load it after drafting, as the checklist a critic reviews against. Load it before drafting too, so the first draft aims at the bar instead of the average.
- **Update cadence:** Add a line to the rejection log every time you turn down a draft. Promote a log entry to a rule once it shows up twice.

Owner: [Name, Title]
Company: [Company Name]

Last updated: [YYYY-MM-DD]

The examples come from Marlowe & Voss Family Law, the fictional firm in [`/11-marketing-plan/`](../../11-marketing-plan/EXAMPLE-marlowe-voss-family-law.md). Replace them with your own.

## Taste is not performance, and it is not voice

- **Not performance.** `/09-data/TOP-PERFORMERS.md` tells you what got clicks. Taste tells you what you would be proud to have written. Use performance to find candidates and taste to decide.
- **Not voice.** `/02-voice/VOICE.md` covers how the brand sounds. Taste covers whether the thinking is any good.

## 2. My rules

Each rule is something a reviewer can check. Write the reason so the rule can bend correctly when a case does not fit.

| Rule | Why | How to check a draft |
|---|---|---|
| [Rule] | [Reason] | [Test] |

Starter rules to replace or keep:

| Rule | Why | How to check a draft |
|---|---|---|
| One sharp idea beats a broad summary. | Summaries are what an AI answer already gives the reader for free. | Can you state the piece's one idea in a sentence? |
| A bold claim needs a mechanism or a number under it. | Without one, a contrarian take reads as attention-seeking. | Underline each claim. Is there a "because" or a figure next to it? |
| Lead with the reader's situation, not our method. | The reader cares about their problem before they care how we solve it. | Does the first paragraph mention us or them? |
| The reader can explain it to someone else afterward. | If they cannot repeat it, it did not land. ([Kieran Flanagan's test](https://www.kieranflanagan.io/p/the-creative-context-os-how-to-stop)) | Ten minutes after reading, could the reader explain the core idea to a colleague? |

## 3. The pre-publish tests

> Example for Marlowe & Voss: Would a parent who has never been to court understand every sentence? Is there one thing they can do before their next hearing? Does anything sound like it came off another firm's site?

## 4. Rejection log

Every rejected or heavily rewritten draft goes here. When the same reason shows up twice, promote it to a rule in section 2.

| Date | What got rejected | What I said | The rule it implies |
|---|---|---|---|
| [YYYY-MM-DD] | [Draft or line] | "[Your words]" | [Rule] |

> Example: a headline that read "Custody, Simplified." Dana's note: "Simplified for who? Say what they get." Implied rule: headlines name the reader's outcome, never a clever label.
