## ICP versus audience

Your ICP and your audience overlap, but they answer different questions. The ICP describes the people most likely to buy and stay. This file describes how those people read: their mood when they find you, what they have heard a hundred times, and what makes them trust a stranger. A persona can be accurate and still produce writing nobody finishes. This file is the fix.

## How to keep it real

- **Study the people who engage.** Look at who replies, comments, saves, and shares. Note what they have in common with your five people and where they differ.
- **Ask.** One question at the end of an email ("what's the hardest part of this for you right now?") fills this file faster than any research tool.
- **Look outside.** Read what your reader reads elsewhere. The things they are tired of hearing usually come from there.
