# Creator

Part of M10: The Marketing Foundation 10 by imFORZA. One of the three [creative context](README.md) files.

## How to use this file

- **What goes in:** How one specific person thinks and writes when the work is going well. Patterns, each backed by a short quote from real work.
- **How agents read it:** Load it with `/02-voice/VOICE.md` before any writing task under this person's name. `VOICE.md` sets the brand's tone. This file tells the agent how this person builds an argument.
- **Update cadence:** Re-read it every quarter against the latest work. Update it when a new piece joins the training set.

Company: [Company Name]

## 2. How I build an argument

The order you move in, from first line to last. Write it as steps, with a quote from a training-set piece for each one.

1. [Step]. Evidence: "[short quote]"
2. [Step]. Evidence: "[short quote]"
3. [Step]. Evidence: "[short quote]"

> Example: (1) Name the fear the client walked in with. (2) Say what actually happens in court, plainly. (3) Give one thing they can do before the next hearing. Evidence: "You're worried the judge will only hear his side. Here's what the judge actually has in front of her."

## 3. How I introduce an idea

How your openings work. The first line, and what you do in the first three sentences.

- **I open with:** [a situation, a number, a client's words, a mistake, a question]
- **I never open with:** [what you avoid]
- Evidence: "[an opening line from the training set]"

> Example: I open with the moment a client realizes something is at stake. I never open with a statute or a definition. "You usually find out what 'temporary orders' means the week you need one."

## 9. If you do not have enough work yet

Use copywork. Pick two or three writers whose work you want yours to feel like. Copy a few of their best pieces out by hand to feel how they are built, then write your own piece on your own subject using the same structure. Fill in this file from what you wrote, not from what they wrote. Once a handful of your own pieces exist, swap them into the training set.
