# Campaign Recipe: Product Launch

Part of M10: The Marketing Foundation 10 by imFORZA.

## Purpose

A multi-touch campaign to launch a new product, service, or major feature. Designed for a 6-week window: 2 weeks of prep, 2 weeks of build-up, 2 weeks of live.

## Inputs required

- Confirmed launch date.
- Offer approved in `/05-offer/OFFER.md`.
- Landing page approved per `/10-brand-brain/templates/landing-page.md`.
- Proof points ready in `/08-content/PROOF-CORPUS.md`.
- Metrics in `/09-data/ANALYTICS.md` with conversion events defined.

## Campaign objectives

- Top-funnel: 20,000 impressions to the target persona across paid and organic.
- Mid-funnel: 1,000 visits to the launch landing page.
- Bottom-funnel: 60 booked calls.
- Close: 6 signed engagements within 30 days of launch.

Numbers are examples. Replace with your targets.

## Timeline

### Weeks minus 2 and minus 1 (prep)

- Finalize offer, pricing, and asset list.
- Draft landing page.
- Record one customer video testimonial.
- Draft the press or announcement text.
- Align sales team on messaging.
- Prepare launch-day dashboard in `/09-data/ANALYTICS.md`.

### Week 1 (seed)

- Day 1: Private founder post on LinkedIn, "we are about to ship X."
- Day 2: Newsletter teaser to list.
- Day 3: Thread on X with a contrarian claim.
- Day 5: Podcast episode with the founder on the backstory of the launch.
- Day 7: Partner outreach, 10 hand-picked people.

### Week 2 (launch)

- Day 8: Public landing page goes live, announcement posts on LinkedIn, X, and newsletter.
- Day 9: Customer video testimonial posted on LinkedIn.
- Day 10: Pillar blog post goes live with the full argument.
- Day 11: Quora answer seeded on a high-traffic question.
- Day 12: Founder does one podcast guest appearance in the target audience.
- Day 14: Week 2 recap post summarizing early traction.

### Week 3 (momentum)

- Day 15: Named customer case study goes live on the blog.
- Day 16: Second LinkedIn post with lessons from the first week.
- Day 17: Newsletter with the launch recap and a 1:1 invitation.
- Day 18: Paid ad variants go live on LinkedIn and Google.
- Day 21: One direct-mail hand-written note to 10 top ICP targets.

### Weeks 4 and 5 (compound)

- Blog spokes supporting the pillar publish twice per week.
- Sales team sends a first-person outreach sequence referencing the launch.
- Founder continues daily LinkedIn posts, shifting from "we shipped this" to "here is what we are learning."
- Partner outreach follow-ups.

### Week 6 (wrap)

- Publish a numbers post with real launch results, honest about misses.
- Email list: "what we learned," no ask.
- Archive assets into `/08-content/CONTENT-LIBRARY.md`.
- Promote winners to `/09-data/TOP-PERFORMERS.md`.

## Decision points

- If week-2 landing page conversion is under 2 percent, adjust hero headline before week 3.
- If week-3 paid CAC is over 1.5x target, pause variants and rewrite creative.
- If week-4 pipeline is under target, add a second founder letter in week 5.

## Success checkpoint

Every Friday for six weeks, review the dashboard. If off track, re-brief the variant that is underperforming. Do not redesign the campaign mid-run.

## Worked example

The example pattern above was used to launch the imFORZA Marketing Foundation Audit. Week-2 landing page conversion: 3.8 percent. Week-6 booked calls: 71. Week-6 signed engagements: 7.
