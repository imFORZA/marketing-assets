# 08, Content Library, Proof Corpus, and Editorial Calendar

The Surface Layer closes here. The site is mapped. The schema is wired. Now you fill the site and the channels with substance.

## What this layer does

Three files and one template fill this folder. Together they hold every piece of content you produce, every testimonial you can quote, and every week of your publishing plan.

- `CONTENT-LIBRARY.md` is the inventory of every asset: blog posts, guides, videos, podcasts, webinars, lead magnets, case studies. If it is not in the library, it does not exist.
- `EDITORIAL-CALENDAR.md` is the 90-day rolling plan. Weeks, channels, content types, owners, and status.
- `PROOF-CORPUS.md` is the testimonial, case-study, review, and UGC bank. Agents cite from this file.
- `templates/case-study-template.md` is the fill-in-the-blank outline for every case study.

## Files in this folder

- `CONTENT-LIBRARY.md`
- `EDITORIAL-CALENDAR.md`
- `PROOF-CORPUS.md`
- `templates/case-study-template.md`

## Build order inside this layer

1. Start with `CONTENT-LIBRARY.md`. Inventory what already exists. Archive what is dead. Refresh what is stale.
2. Fill in `PROOF-CORPUS.md`. Every claim you want to make needs a cited proof point.
3. Plan `EDITORIAL-CALENDAR.md` 90 days out.
4. Use `case-study-template.md` every time a customer story is ready.

## What this folder feeds into

- `09-data` measures the performance of everything in the content library.
- `10-brand-brain` loads proof corpus and editorial rhythms into agent workflows.
