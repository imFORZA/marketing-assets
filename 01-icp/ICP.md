# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Part of M10: The Marketing Foundation 10 by imFORZA.

## How to use this file

- **What goes in:** A written description of the person and the company most likely to buy from you, renew, and refer. Names of roles, sizes of companies, problems, triggers, objections, and the exact words the buyer uses.
- **How agents read it:** AI agents load this file as the first block of context in any content task. It answers "who am I writing for" before the agent writes a single word.
- **Update cadence:** Reread quarterly. Rewrite annually, or after every 10 closed deals, whichever comes first.

## AI-agent-ready checklist

- [ ] Machine-readable: every field is plain text under a predictable heading.
- [ ] Structured: one persona per H2 section, firmographics, JTBD, triggers, pain, objections, disqualifiers.
- [ ] Single source of truth: no duplicate ICP files in other folders.
- [ ] Versioned: a "Last updated" date at the top.
- [ ] Cite-able: any agent output sourced from this file includes a reference to `/01-icp/ICP.md`.

## Worked Example

### Primary Persona: Brenda Alvarez, Director of Operations, Northstar Home Services

Brenda runs operations at a 45-person residential HVAC and plumbing company in Austin. She manages dispatch, inventory, and field-tech scheduling. She reports to the owner. She has 12 years in the industry. She is the person every tech texts when a job goes sideways.

**Firmographics**
- Company size: 45 employees, 28 in the field.
- Revenue: $8.5M annual.
- Industry: residential HVAC, plumbing, and electrical.
- Geography: Austin metro, expanding to San Antonio.
- Business model: project-based with recurring maintenance plans.
- Tech stack: ServiceTitan, QuickBooks, Gmail, Google Ads, a legacy WordPress site.

**Jobs to Be Done**
1. When I take on a marketing vendor, I want to know what they will deliver each month, so I can stop explaining the same thing to the owner every quarter.
2. When I look at leads, I want to know which channel drove each one, so I can cut the channels that do not work.
3. When I publish content, I want it to show up in Google and ChatGPT for the searches my buyers actually run, so I can stop relying on paid ads.

**Trigger Events**
- Hired a new sales manager who is demanding better lead flow.
- Lost a $120K commercial account to a competitor that ranked #1 for "Austin commercial HVAC contractor."
- Google Ads cost per lead went up 38 percent year over year.

**Pain Points**
1. Lead volume is unpredictable month to month, costing an estimated $40K in idle tech hours per quarter.
2. The website has not been updated in four years and does not rank for the services that drive the most margin.
3. The marketing agency sends reports full of impressions and clicks, but nobody can tie those to booked jobs.

**Top Objection**
"We tried an agency last year and got nothing. How is this different."

**Rebuttal**
We publish a shared dashboard that ties every asset to booked revenue. We also commit to a 60-day asset audit before we propose anything, so you see the plan before you sign.
