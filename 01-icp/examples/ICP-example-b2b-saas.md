# ICP Example: B2B SaaS, "Cypress Coffee Co. Roastery Ops Platform"

Fictional company, real shape. Use this as a reference when you fill in your own ICP.

## Primary Persona: Marcus Chen, Head of Operations, mid-sized specialty coffee roaster

Marcus is 38, runs ops for a 60-person specialty coffee roaster with five wholesale accounts and a direct-to-consumer subscription business. He reports to the founder, manages production scheduling, inventory, and fulfillment. He is the person who gets paged when a wholesale order ships late or when the green coffee inventory is off.

### Firmographics

- Company size: 60 employees.
- Revenue: $14M annual, 60 percent DTC, 40 percent wholesale.
- Industry: specialty coffee roasting and subscription retail.
- Geography: Pacific Northwest, ships in all 50 states.
- Business model: subscription plus wholesale plus retail café.
- Tech stack: Shopify Plus, NetSuite, Klaviyo, Google Workspace, a home-grown roast scheduling tool in Airtable.

### Demographics

- Title: Head of Operations.
- Seniority: Director, two reports and a production team of 12.
- Tenure: 5 years at this company, 12 years in specialty coffee.
- Reports to: Founder and COO.

### Psychographics

- Pragmatic. Does not chase shiny tools. Trusts recommendations from peers in the Specialty Coffee Association.
- Decision style: evaluates on total cost of ownership including switching costs.
- Values: reliability, direct access to the vendor team, honest roadmaps.
- Fears: a vendor that sells hard and ghosts during implementation.
- Aspirations: stop firefighting and get back to working on quality.

### Jobs to Be Done

1. When wholesale orders spike in Q4, I want to scale production scheduling without overtime blowing up my P&L, so I can hit margin targets.
2. When I forecast green coffee purchases, I want demand signals from DTC and wholesale in one view, so I can avoid either shortages or dead stock.
3. When leadership asks for operational reports, I want them generated automatically, so I can stop pulling all-nighters before board meetings.

### Trigger Events

- Just lost a $400K wholesale account over a chronic late-shipment pattern.
- Founder hired a new VP of Finance who is demanding cleaner data.
- Closed a Series A and needs to professionalize operations before Series B.

### Pain Points

1. Roast scheduling lives in Airtable and Google Sheets. Costs an estimated 8 hours per week across the ops team.
2. No single view of DTC plus wholesale demand. Led to $180K in dead green coffee last year.
3. Wholesale SLA reporting is manual. Lost the $400K account partly because they could not produce an on-time delivery report.

### Objections and Rebuttals

| Objection | Who raises it | Rebuttal | Proof point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| We can build this in Airtable | Marcus himself | Airtable will scale until volume breaks it. Then the rebuild cost is bigger than the vendor cost. | Benchmark from a peer roaster. |
| Your pricing is higher than competitor X | CFO | We ship two integrations competitor X does not. Total cost of ownership is lower. | Side-by-side TCO spreadsheet. |
| We are too small for this | Founder | We have three customers at $8M to $12M using the same tier. | Reference list. |

### Disqualifiers

- Roasters under $3M revenue: not enough volume to justify platform cost.
- Pure DTC without wholesale: half the platform value is unused.
- Roasters that refuse to give up their Airtable workflows: implementation will fail.

### Budget Range

- Current spend on this problem: $25K per year split across tools and overtime.
- Expected spend on our platform: $36K to $60K per year.
- Budget owner: CFO, with Marcus as the decision maker up to $50K.

### Decision Criteria

1. Proven wholesale SLA reporting.
2. Native integration with NetSuite and Shopify Plus.
3. Direct access to the implementation team.
4. Reference customers in specialty coffee.
5. Transparent pricing with no hidden per-user seats.

### Content Consumption Habits

- Newsletters: Daily Coffee News, Perfect Daily Grind, Barista Hustle, the SCA weekly update.
- Podcasts: Coffee People, A Matter of Life and Coffee.
- Communities: SCA member forum, a private Slack for operations leads at mid-sized roasters.
- Events: SCA Expo, Specialty Coffee Expo, regional ops roundtables.
- Social: LinkedIn more than X. Instagram for peer watching.
- Search: Google first, ChatGPT second, peer DM third.

### Voice-of-Customer Snippets

- "I do not have time to learn another dashboard. If your tool does not show me what I need in 30 seconds on a Monday morning, it is out.", Discovery call, March 2026.
- "The last vendor I bought from sent me a 60-page onboarding doc. I need a human.", Churn interview, November 2025.
- "We lost Starbucks Reserve because we could not show them a clean on-time delivery report. I never want that to happen again.", Sales call, January 2026.
