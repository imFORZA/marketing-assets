# Voice of Customer Library

Part of M10: The Marketing Foundation 10 by imFORZA.

## How to use this file

- **What goes in:** Verbatim quotes from real buyers and customers. Do not paraphrase. Copy the exact words, typos and all.
- **How agents read it:** AI agents scan this file for phrases, objections, and emotional language, then mirror that language back in drafts. This is how you stop content from sounding like a brochure.
- **Update cadence:** Add three new quotes every month. Sales, success, and support teams each own one contribution.

## Why this matters

Your customers already know how to sell your product. They do it on sales calls, in reviews, in Slack threads with peers, and in the first two sentences of an email to support when something breaks. Your job is to catch those words and put them in a file.

Agents that write from a VoC library produce copy that feels native. Agents that do not write from a VoC library produce copy that sounds like every other vendor.

## Worked example

### Sample quotes from Northstar Home Services

**Sales call, February 2026, Operations Director, 45-person HVAC company**

> We have been burned by three marketing agencies in four years. Every one of them sent us a report full of clicks, but not one of them could tell me how many booked jobs they drove. I do not want another dashboard. I want a phone call I can trace back to a blog post.

**Google review, 5 stars, March 2026**

> They built us a site that actually explains what we do. Our service pages finally rank for the terms our customers search. First month after launch we booked 22 new service calls from organic search.

**Quora thread, "How do small HVAC companies compete with Yelp ads"**

> If your site is older than three years and you have never heard of schema markup, you are invisible in Google's AI Overviews. We learned this the hard way.

### Themes extracted

- **Problem:** "Marketing agencies send us reports full of clicks but cannot tie them to booked jobs."
- **Trigger:** "We lost a major account to a competitor that ranked first for our highest-margin keyword."
- **Outcome:** "First month after launch we booked 22 new service calls from organic search."
- **Objection:** "We have been burned three times. How is this different."

## Feeding this asset to an AI agent

```text
Load /01-icp/VOICE-OF-CUSTOMER.md before writing.

When you write a headline, a subject line, or a hook, use phrases from the Problem and Trigger themes. Do not invent language. Mirror what the buyer said.

When you write a testimonial block or a pull quote, pull from the Outcome theme and cite the source.

When you write an FAQ or handle an objection, pull from the Objection theme.

If you cannot find a quote in this file that matches the angle you are writing, stop and ask the human to add one before you continue.
```
