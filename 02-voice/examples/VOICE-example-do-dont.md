# Voice Example: Do vs. Do-Not

Use these side-by-side rewrites as reference. The goal is not to memorize. The goal is to tune your ear.

Part of M10: The Marketing Foundation 10 by imFORZA.

## Example 1: Homepage hero

**Do not:**
> In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, our world-class marketing platform seamlessly integrates with your existing stack to unlock unprecedented growth for forward-thinking SMBs.

**Do:**
> Write down your marketing foundation once. Every AI tool you buy gets better. Every campaign you ship gets faster. Every team you hire gets smarter.

## Example 2: Service page intro

**Do not:**
> Our team of seasoned experts leverages cutting-edge AI to drive meaningful outcomes across the entire customer journey.

**Do:**
> We write down the ten assets your business needs before any AI agent can do useful work for you. When we are done, your team has a marketing system a new hire or a new tool can load in 15 minutes.

## Example 3: Blog post opening

**Do not:**
> In an era of rapid technological disruption, businesses are increasingly recognizing the importance of robust marketing foundations. Many organizations are now pivoting toward AI-first strategies to remain competitive.

**Do:**
> A client called us on a Tuesday morning. Their ChatGPT assistant had just published a blog post under their name. It was well written, on topic, and three paragraphs in, it recommended a competitor. This is what happens when you hand an agent the keys without handing it the map.

## Example 4: Email subject line

**Do not:**
> Unlock Your Business's Potential With Our Revolutionary Platform

**Do:**
> the 10 files your AI assistant needs before it writes anything for you

## Example 5: LinkedIn post

**Do not:**
> Excited to announce that we're partnering with industry leaders to revolutionize how SMBs leverage AI in their marketing efforts. This game-changing collaboration will unlock unprecedented value for our customers.

**Do:**
> We just shipped a free template pack for small businesses who want their marketing foundation to work with AI agents.
>
> Ten folders. One per asset. Fork it, fill it in, paste it into Claude or ChatGPT, and stop re-explaining your brand every time a new tool shows up.
>
> Link below. No email gate.

## Example 6: Case study pull quote

**Do not:**
> "We have achieved significant improvements across all marketing KPIs since partnering with the imFORZA team."

**Do:**
> "First month after launch we booked 22 new service calls from organic search. I have never had a report I trusted before this one.", Brenda Alvarez, Director of Operations, Northstar Home Services

## Example 7: CTA

**Do not:**
> Discover how our innovative solutions can transform your business today!

**Do:**
> Book a 30-minute audit. We will show you which of the ten assets you already have, and which ones you do not.

## Example 8: FAQ

**Do not:**
> Q: Can we work with you if we're a small business?
> A: Absolutely! We pride ourselves on our ability to support SMBs of all shapes and sizes with our world-class capabilities.

**Do:**
> **Q: Is this a fit if we have five employees and no in-house marketing team?**
>
> A: Yes if you are the operator and willing to spend 4 to 6 hours writing down the foundation with us. No if you are looking for someone to run your marketing without your input. We write the system. You own it.

## Example 9: Headline on a data point

**Do not:**
> Studies show that AI-driven companies experience significantly higher growth rates.

**Do:**
> 73 percent of small and growing businesses report at least one AI tool in their marketing workflow in 2026. Only 12 percent say the output is consistent. That gap is the foundation gap.

## Example 10: Voice attribution line

**Do not:**
> This blog post was written by the imFORZA content team.

**Do:**
> Written by the imFORZA team. We are a marketing foundation practice for small and growing businesses. We publish our templates because we think the category should be open.

## What to take from these examples

- Lead with the claim, the number, the reader, or the scene. Never with "in today's."
- Name people, companies, tools, and dollar amounts.
- Use short sentences on purpose.
- Write like you talk to the buyer on a phone call, not like you present at a conference.
- If a sentence would embarrass you on a sales call, cut it.
