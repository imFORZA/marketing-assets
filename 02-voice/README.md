# 02, Brand Voice, Tone, and Terminology

The Identity Layer continues. In `01-icp` you wrote down who you serve. Here you write down how you talk to them.

## What this layer does

This folder tells any agent, any writer, and any designer the exact voice to use. It covers point of view, tone, sentence rules, punctuation, formatting, preferred and banned words, and the compliance guardrails that cannot be crossed.

Without these files, every asset sounds like whoever wrote it last. With these files, everything your company publishes sounds like one person wrote it, even when six different agents and three humans are drafting at once.

## Files in this folder

- `VOICE.md`, The voice and tone system. Point of view, brand archetype, voice attributes with do and do-not examples, tone modulation by channel, sentence and punctuation rules.
- `TERMINOLOGY.md`, The preferred and banned word list. Definitions, rationale, regionalisms, industry language.
- `COMPLIANCE.md`, The guardrails. What you can claim and what you cannot. Required disclaimers. Regulated-industry rules.
- `examples/VOICE-example-do-dont.md`, Worked examples showing what the voice looks like on a page.

## Build order inside this layer

1. Write `VOICE.md` first. Without voice, terminology has no anchor.
2. Fill in `TERMINOLOGY.md`. Start with the 10 words you most often get wrong and the 10 words you refuse to use.
3. Finish with `COMPLIANCE.md`. If you are in a regulated industry, run this past legal counsel before any agent writes copy that will be published.

## What this folder feeds into

- `03-visual` uses voice to calibrate visual tone (loud vs. quiet, playful vs. serious).
- `07-website` inherits voice for navigation, page copy, and microcopy.
- `08-content` uses voice across every piece published.
- `10-brand-brain` bakes voice into every system prompt and every agent persona.
