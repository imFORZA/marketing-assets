# Offer, Positioning, and Packaging

Part of M10: The Marketing Foundation 10 by imFORZA.

## How to use this file

- **What goes in:** Every offer you sell, described the same way every time. Name, promise, inclusions, timeline, pricing, outcomes, proof, and who it is for.
- **How agents read it:** Agents load this file before drafting any sales page, proposal, email, or pitch. If an offer is not in this file, the agent refuses to sell it.
- **Update cadence:** Review quarterly. Update the day you change pricing or rename an offer.

## Positioning statement

One paragraph. Use the classic format.

> For [primary persona] who [primary JTBD], [Company] is the [category] that [unique value]. Unlike [main alternative], we [key differentiator].

Example placeholder:

> For operators at small and growing businesses who want their marketing to compound instead of restart every quarter, imFORZA is the marketing foundation practice that writes your ICP, voice, offer, and content system into a set of files every AI agent can load. Unlike agencies that sell point services, we sell the foundation that makes every vendor, tool, and agent more useful the day after we leave.

## Pricing principles

- We publish pricing unless a category norm forbids it.
- We price on value delivered, not hours spent.
- We never quote in a sales call without naming a range.
- We never sign an engagement without a scope document, a change-order process, and an out clause.

## Worked example: Marketing Foundation Audit (sample imFORZA offer)

**Category:** Fixed-scope consulting engagement.
**One-sentence description:** A 60-day audit that produces your filled-in Marketing Foundation 10 repo, ready for any AI agent to load.

**Who it is for**

- Small and growing businesses under 250 employees.
- Operators with at least one person accountable for marketing inside the company.
- Teams already using one AI tool for content, ads, or sales and frustrated with inconsistent output.

**What is included**

- 8 working sessions, 90 minutes each.
- Voice-of-customer interviews, 6 people.
- Filled-in ICP, Voice, Offer, and Competitive files.
- Visual brand audit and token file.
- Content inventory of existing assets.
- Initial keyword and topic cluster plan.
- Brand Brain system prompt, tested in Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini.
- Recorded walkthrough the team can replay when a new hire starts.

**Timeline**

- Kickoff to first deliverable: week 2.
- Total engagement: 60 days.

**Pricing**

- $24,000 flat fee, 50 percent at kickoff, 50 percent at delivery.

**Expected outcomes**

- A filled-in M10 repo every new AI tool can load in 15 minutes.
- A 30 to 60 percent reduction in back-and-forth on first-draft content.
- A named reduction in time-to-launch for the next campaign.

**Proof**

- Reference: Northstar Home Services, 47 organic leads in month 1 after delivery.
- Reference: Kestrel Family Medicine, 42 percent lift in bookings in 90 days.
- Data: our client benchmark set of 18 small-and-growing-business engagements.

## Feeding this asset to an AI agent

```text
Load /05-offer/OFFER.md before writing any sales, pricing, or proposal content.

When you draft:
1. Use the positioning statement verbatim as the anchor.
2. Quote the promises exactly. Do not invent new promises.
3. Use only offers listed in this file. If a prospect asks for something not listed, flag it for a human.
4. Match pricing to the exact numbers here. If pricing is not published, say "priced based on scope" and flag.
5. Reference proof points by name, not by generalization.

Cite /05-offer/OFFER.md in every draft.
```
