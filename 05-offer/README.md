# 05, Offer, Positioning, and Competitive Intelligence

The Market Layer opens here. The Identity Layer said who you are and how you look. This layer says what you sell, to whom, and against whom.

## What this layer does

This folder is the single source of truth for your offer and your market context. It covers productized offers, packaging tiers, promise statements, differentiation, competitor analysis, and win-loss learning.

Every agent that writes a sales email, a pricing page, a proposal, or a competitor-aware landing page reads this folder first.

## Files in this folder

- `OFFER.md`, Productized offer catalog. Each offer has a name, deliverables, timeline, outcomes, pricing, and proof.
- `COMPETITORS.md`, Competitor map. Direct, indirect, substitute. Per-competitor breakdown and battle cards.
- `WIN-LOSS.md`, Template for post-deal interviews. What made buyers choose us or someone else.

## Build order inside this layer

1. Write `OFFER.md`. If you cannot name the offer, you cannot sell it or prompt an agent to describe it.
2. Fill in `COMPETITORS.md`. Name names.
3. Write `WIN-LOSS.md` as a rolling log. Review every closed deal.

## What this folder feeds into

- `06-seo` uses offer language to prioritize commercial keywords.
- `07-website` uses offer content on the services and pricing pages.
- `08-content` uses offers and competitor insight for comparison and pillar content.
- `10-brand-brain` loads offer and competitor context into every sales-related system prompt.
